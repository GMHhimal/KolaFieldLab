# KolaCare user guide

## 1. Getting started
1. Open KolaCare. Swipe through the three introduction pages (or tap **Skip**).
2. Choose the crops you grow and tap **Get started**. Allow location access if you want
   local weather alerts and map pins (the app still works without it, using Colombo weather).
3. To explore quickly, tap **Demo garden** on the Home screen.

## 2. Scanning a leaf
1. Open the **Scan** tab.
2. Tap **Take photo** (on an iPhone) or **Photo library**. In the Simulator, use **Try a sample**.
3. Fill the frame with one leaf, in daylight, on a plain background.
4. Wait about two seconds while KolaCare isolates the leaf, measures damaged tissue and matches disease patterns.
5. On the **Diagnosis** screen:
   * Pinch, drag or double-tap the photo to zoom. Toggle **Show lesion map** to see yellowing (amber) and dead tissue (red).
   * Read the health score, confidence and % of leaf affected.
   * Orange warnings explain how to retake a poor photo.
   * **What to do now** lists the first treatment steps; **Full treatment guide** opens the library entry.
6. Choose the plant (or **New plant**), add notes, keep **Remind me to re-scan** on, and tap **Save diagnosis**.

## 3. Managing your garden
* **Garden** tab → **+** to add a plant: name, crop, plot, planting date, photo and location
  (tap the map or **Use my location**).
* Filter by crop with the chips; sort with the ↕ button; search by name or plot.
* Long-press a card for **Scan this plant**, **Edit** and **Delete**.
* Open a plant to see its health trend chart (drag across it to inspect a point), scan timeline and location.
* Open a scan to tick off treatment steps; progress is saved instantly.

## 4. Weather disease alerts (Home)
* The **Disease risk · next 48 h** card shows temperature, humidity and leaf-wet hours for your location.
* Diseases are ranked **High / Moderate / Low** from the number of hours that suit them. Tap one for its guide.
* Pull down to refresh. When offline, the last saved forecast is shown with a notice.

## 5. Field map
* Pins are coloured by the plant's latest health. Severe plants pulse.
* Circles show **spread zones** — check neighbouring plots inside them first.
* Use the layers button to switch satellite view and spread zones, or show all plants.

## 6. Library
* Search by disease name, symptom ("concentric rings") or pathogen.
* Filter by crop or by type (fungal, bacterial, viral, pest…).
* Switch between **Organic & cultural** and **Chemical** treatments. Use **Call 1920** for the DOA advisory centre.

## 7. Settings
Appearance (System/Light/Dark), colour theme (Paddy green, Tea estate, Cinnamon), language
(iOS Settings › KolaCare › Language: English, සිංහල, தமிழ்), alert crops, reminder interval,
lesion map default, model status, CSV export, demo data and erase all data.

## 8. Accessibility
KolaCare supports VoiceOver labels for every control and chart, Dynamic Type up to the largest
accessibility sizes, Dark Mode, Reduce Motion (animations become fades) and 44-point touch targets.
